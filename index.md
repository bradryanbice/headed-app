# Headed

**See your whole trip before you drive it.**

Headed is a journey-overview companion for Apple Maps. Instead of a stream of upcoming turns, you
get the big picture: your whole route on one map, exactly where you are on it, how much is left,
and what's nearby — all at a glance.

When you actually need turn-by-turn guidance, Headed hands off to Apple Maps with one tap. It
doesn't try to replace it. **Turn-by-turn is Apple Maps' job. Seeing the whole trip is Headed's.**

## What it does

- **Your entire route on one map**, rendered clearly, with live GPS progress as you drive.
- **Alternate routes** to compare before you commit.
- **Ahead** — the next food, restroom, gas, or EV charging *ahead of you on your route*, without
  leaving it.
- **Arrival** — parking, food, and services around your destination, so you know where you're
  putting the car before you get there.
- **Weather along the route** — a precipitation radar layer, forecasts timed to when you'll
  actually arrive, and severe-weather alerts.
- **A trip summary when you arrive**, ready to share.

## Privacy, briefly

No account. No login. No tracking, no ads, no analytics. Everything Headed remembers about your
trips — recent destinations, saved places, preferences — stays on your device. Nothing is uploaded
anywhere, because there's no server to upload it to.

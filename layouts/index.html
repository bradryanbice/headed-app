The two that matter

Ahead and Arrival

Two searches, two different questions. Most map apps only answer one of them — and once you're moving, it's the wrong one.

Ahead

What's coming up, on the road you're already on

Headed searches a corridor along your route, not a circle around your car. So the gas station it finds is one you'll actually pass — not one four miles back the way you came.

One row each for food, restroom, gas, and charging, with the distance ahead. Open a row for more options, or set preferred brands so your chain comes first.

  • Distances measured forward along the route, not as the crow flies
  • EV charging shows real power ratings, not just a pin
  • Never leaves the map — the route stays on screen behind it

Arrival

Where the car goes, before you're circling the block

Arrival searches around your destination — because a radius around a moving car goes stale the moment you drive on, and parking is an arrival problem, not a mid-drive one.

Ask it from an hour out and the answers are still right when you get there. Pin the ones you like and they stay on the map as you approach.

  • Distances read from where you're going, not where you are
  • Parking, food, restrooms, gas, and charging around the destination
  • Pinned places persist on the route map for the rest of the drive

Done

Hand off whenever you want turns

Headed doesn't try to replace Apple Maps. One tap sends the route across for turn-by-turn, stops included. Seeing the whole trip is Headed's job; the turns are Apple's.

Privacy

Everything stays on your phone

Headed has no server. That isn't a policy position — there is genuinely nowhere for your trips to go.

  • No account, no login, nothing to sign up for.
  • No analytics, no ad tracking, no third-party SDKs in the app.
  • Saved places, recent destinations, and preferences live on your device only.
  • Delete the app and it's all gone — there was never a copy anywhere else.

Location goes to Apple for routing and weather, and a map area goes to RainViewer and Open Charge Map when you use those features. The policy says exactly what each one sees.
